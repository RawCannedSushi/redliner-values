Accessibility statement

Updated October 6, 2026

I want archives® Redliner Values to be usable by everyone, including people who use a keyboard, screen reader, or other assistive technology. I'm using WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a goal. The site hasn't had a full accessibility audit yet, so I can't say it meets that standard.

What's here now

You can switch between light and dark modes or choose a compressed layout. The site also follows your device's reduced motion setting.

What still needs checking

The interactive value chart and Skin Gallery haven't been fully checked with screen readers or other assistive technology.

Feedback and help

If something stops you from using the site or getting the information you need, please tell me through the archives® Discord or open a GitHub issue. Tell me which page you were on, what you were trying to do, and what happened. I'll use reports to guide fixes.
