Privacy & storage

Effective October 7, 2026

What this site stores

Redliner Values has no user accounts, advertising, or site analytics. Your inventory, display preferences, rarity preference, and cached value data are stored in your browser. They remain on that browser and device unless you export them or choose the optional Google Drive inventory sync.

The public Scam List shows account names, IDs, explanations, and notes maintained in the community sheet. Searching that list happens in your browser; your search text is not sent to the site server.

Optional Google Drive inventory sync

Google sync requests only the drive.appdata permission. This lets the app create, read, update, and delete files in this app's private Google Drive application-data folder. Redliner uses that access for its inventory file. It does not grant access to browse, read, or change your regular Google Drive files. The app does not request your Google profile, name, or email address.

The Google access token is kept temporarily in the open page's memory. It is sent only to Google APIs and is not stored by the Redliner server. The inventory file is used only to provide the synchronization you request. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, shared with data brokers, or used to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

You can disconnect Google on this page without deleting either copy. While connected, you can also permanently delete the Google Drive copy from the sync information dialog; the local browser copy remains and sync disconnects. Local edits after deletion do not recreate the Google file unless you explicitly reconnect. A request already received by Google may finish even if you disconnect before its response arrives. You can revoke the app's permission from your Google Account's third-party connections.

Hosting and third-party services

Cloudflare hosts the site and API and may process ordinary request data, including IP address, browser information, requested URL, time, and security logs. Cloudflare D1 stores public skin-value history, not user inventories. The site server reads current values and the Scam List from Google Sheets. Google also receives requests when the Google font or optional Drive synchronization feature is used. Their handling is governed by their own policies.

Links to Discord, GitHub, and other sites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Browser storage and choices

Browser storage supports requested features and is not used to track you across unrelated sites. You can clear it through your browser settings. Export your inventory first if you want to retain it. Private browsing, a direct Cloudflare visit, and a Google Sites embed may have separate storage.

Children's information

The site does not ask for a name, age, email address, chat message, or other user-submitted personal information. Do not submit personal information through inventory names or support messages.

Questions

Privacy questions can be directed to the Archives community through the Archives Discord. This policy will be updated before materially different data collection is added.
