# Your data and safety

Your inventory stays in your browser unless you choose to sync it with Google. It isn't sent to the site's server. There's no site account to make, and I haven't added ads or visitor analytics.

## If you use Google sync

Google sync only gets access to this app's private folder in your Drive. It can't read or change your regular Drive files. You sign in through Google, and the site never gets your Google password.

Your inventory goes directly to Google. The temporary access token stays in the open page and isn't saved by the app or sent to the site's server.

After connecting, local changes upload automatically. Click **Sync** on another device to fetch the latest copy. If the copies differ, you'll pick one to keep; sync doesn't combine them. Cancel keeps both copies and pauses uploads.

You can disconnect or delete the Google copy through the sync info button. Disconnecting doesn't delete either inventory or revoke Google's permission. You can remove that permission in your Google Account settings.

## Keep a backup

Use **Export backup** now and then, especially before clearing browser data or switching devices. Sync is useful, but keep a downloaded copy too.

Cloudflare and Google still receive normal connection details, like your IP address, when you use their services through the site. The [privacy policy](https://www.archivesvalues.com/privacy) has the full details.
